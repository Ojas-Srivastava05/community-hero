# Design System: Community Hero (CIVICPULSE AI)

**Codename:** Civic Glass  
**Platform:** Mobile-first PWA (390×844), responsive to tablet  
**Project:** Hyperlocal civic issue reporting — potholes, leaks, streetlights, waste

## Visual Theme

Dark, airy, map-forward civic intelligence. Premium fintech polish meets public-service trust.
Frosted glass panels float above map/content.

## Color Palette

- **Midnight Canvas** `#0B0F14` — App background
- **Elevated Slate** `#151B23` — Card surfaces
- **Frosted Glass** `rgba(255,255,255,0.06)` + 16px blur
- **Civic Pulse Teal** `#14B8A6` — Primary CTA, AI accents
- **Cloud White** `#F8FAFC` — Primary text
- **Mist Gray** `#94A3B8` — Secondary text
- Severity: Critical `#EF4444`, High `#F97316`, Medium `#EAB308`, Low `#22C55E`

## Typography

Inter — Display 28–32px semibold, Body 15–16px, Labels 11–12px uppercase.

- Bottom nav with Report FAB
