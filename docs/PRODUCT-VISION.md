# Product Vision — 30-Second Pitch

Use this script for mentor intros, judge demos, and team alignment. Target: **under 30 seconds** spoken aloud.

---

## The pitch

> **Community Hero** lets any resident fix their neighborhood in four taps.
>
> Snap a photo of a pothole, leak, or broken streetlight — **Gemini AI** classifies it in under three seconds and routes it to the right department.
>
> Neighbors **upvote** to verify; the issue appears on a **live map**; six **AI agents** assign SLA deadlines and prevent duplicates.
>
> Admins resolve with proof photos; everyone sees a **public timeline** — transparent accountability Swachhata never had.
>
> Built on **Google AI Studio**, **Cloud Run**, **Firestore**, and **Maps** — deployed and demo-ready today.

**~28 seconds at moderate pace**

## Elevator variants

### 10 seconds

Community Hero: photograph civic problems, AI triages in 3 seconds, neighbors verify on a live map, agents route to departments — full Google stack on Cloud Run.

### 60 seconds (jury extended)

Indian cities generate millions of civic complaints, but reporting is fragmented and opaque. Swachhata proved citizens will participate — but it lacks AI triage and predictive prevention. Community Hero (CIVICPULSE AI) closes that gap: Gemini 2.5 Flash vision classifies every photo into nine categories with confidence scores; six autonomous agents handle deduplication, department routing, SLA deadlines, and citizen notifications; community upvotes create verification tiers at 1, 3, and 10 votes; predictive hotspots flag recurring problem zones before they escalate. Everything runs on Google AI Studio, Cloud Run, Firestore, Firebase Auth, Cloud Storage, and Maps — with Open311 export for future municipal integration. The result: 80% faster triage, 40% fewer duplicate pins, and a public resolution timeline every citizen can trust.
